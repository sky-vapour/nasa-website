MASS 10,334 kg · STATUS: ETERNAL LANDER

Armstrong and Aldrin blasted off its roof and left the bottom half behind as a launch pad. It still sits in the dust, ladder out, plaque reading “We came in peace.”

HEAD TO HEAD — WHY THE BONEYARDS DIFFER

Two graveyards,
two rules.

🌙 MOON

Perfect preservation

GRAVITY 1.62 m/s²
WIND 0 km/h · FOREVER
PRESERVATION 100%
TEMP SWING ±280°C

Footprints last ~1M years. Golf balls, plaques, crashed stages — vacuum-sealed. Only micrometeorites sand them down, grain by grain.

🔴 MARS

Slow burial

GRAVITY 3.71 m/s²
WIND ≤ 100 km/h DUST
PRESERVATION ~45%
TEMP −125…+20°C

Dust devils wash rovers clean, then planet-wide storms starve them. Phoenix was crushed by ice. InSight became a dune. Mars curates by burying.
